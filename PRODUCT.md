# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Remote-support operators and end users who connect to or receive help from another device by LUODA ID or direct IP address.

## Product Purpose

Provide reliable remote assistance with clear connection, reachability, and device-presence status. Success means users can connect without protocol errors and can trust every status shown in the interface.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, direct, professional.

## Anti-references

Misleading success indicators, color-only status, stale connection state, oversized empty window areas, and decorative controls that interfere with the remote-support workflow.

## Design Principles

- Show verified state rather than optimistic state.
- Keep the primary connection workflow immediate and familiar.
- Use consistent status language across standard and customized clients.
- Keep customized-client windows compact and deterministic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use text together with semantic colors for status and maintain WCAG AA contrast for user-facing text and controls.
